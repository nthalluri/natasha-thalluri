Dakkhini is a contact language that is a result of sustained contact between Hindi-Urdu(Indo-Aryan), and Telugu (Dravidian) for over five centuries. Even though there is a significant overlap in the lexicon, modern Dakkhini is radically different from modern Hindi-Urdu and shows heavy Dravidian influence in its underlying syntax. I argue that syntactic convergence with Telugu has resulted in a broadening of the pronominal paradigm in Dakkhini, giving rise to new third person pronominal forms that are not found in standard Hindi-Urdu.
